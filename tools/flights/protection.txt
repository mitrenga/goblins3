# A flight through the floppy edition's copy protection screen.
#
# The intro chain ends at PROT: eight colour buttons across the top and a
# question in the box below. The answer is different every time, so this
# cannot get *past* it — what it does prove is that the buttons are hotspots
# the engine finds, enters and runs: every click here lands on one.
wait 60
key return          # past the title sequence
wait 60
key return
wait 120

move 20 10          # over the first button, so it gets an "enter"
wait 10
move 60 10          # and along the row, one "leave" and one "enter" each
wait 10
move 100 10
wait 10
move 140 10
wait 10

click 20 10 1       # and then press them
wait 20
click 60 10 1
wait 20
click 100 10 1
wait 20
click 140 10 1
wait 60
